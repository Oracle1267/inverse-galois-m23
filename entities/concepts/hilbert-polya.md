# Hilbert-Polya

## Definition

The Hilbert-Polya idea proposes that the imaginary parts of nontrivial zeta zeros may be eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator.

## Attributes

- Type: concept
- Role: spectral route to RH
- Status: unresolved
