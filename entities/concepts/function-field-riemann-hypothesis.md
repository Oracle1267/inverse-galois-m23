# Function-Field Riemann Hypothesis

## Definition

The function-field Riemann Hypothesis refers to RH-like statements for zeta functions over finite fields. Major forms are proved, including the Weil conjectures through Deligne's work.

## Attributes

- Type: concept
- Role: proved analogue and geometric evidence
- Limitation: does not transfer directly to classical RH over the integers

- Related to [[entities/concepts/riemann-hypothesis]] as one of the strongest structural analogues.
