# Riemann Hypothesis Exploratory Notes

## Summary

[[Riemann Notes]] explores whether the critical line in the Riemann Hypothesis could be interpreted through oscillatory, probabilistic, or quantum-like structure. The current note treats these as exploratory ideas, not established mathematical claims.

## Current Claims

- Classical RH places nontrivial zeros on `Re(s) = 1/2`.
- A literal sinusoidal displacement from the critical line would conflict with the symmetry of the zeta functional equation unless the underlying framework changed.
- Random matrix theory and the Hilbert-Polya idea provide established reasons to compare zeta zeros with spectral or quantum-chaotic systems.
- A safer interpretation is that wave-like behavior may live in spacing, density, correlation, or resonance structure rather than in real-part displacement.

## Confidence

The classical framing and references to random matrix theory and Hilbert-Polya are higher confidence. The sinusoidal or quantum-substrate interpretation is speculative and should remain working memory until supported by stronger analysis.
